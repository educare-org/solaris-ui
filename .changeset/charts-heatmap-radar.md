Add `solaris-heatmap` and `solaris-radar` (`data-display/charts`, phase H5): the heatmap renders a fixed-geometry SVG matrix of rows by columns where cell intensity mixes the primary token over the neutral surface and empty periods paint with the neutral subtle token, with a sticky row gutter under horizontal scroll, per-cell tooltips docked outside the cell on the dominant side and 2D roving-tabindex cells emitting `cellSelect`, plus an optional `showScale` intensity bar; the radar draws one polygon per series on shared axes with a filter-style legend (`Filter by:` title, hover affordance, struck-through hidden labels, stable palette slots, `seriesToggle`) and focusable vertices emitting `vertexSelect` with tooltips docked outside each vertex, growing from the center with a per-series stagger. Both reuse the chart tokens, synthesized summaries, screen-reader tables and empty states, and never mutate consumer input. Showcase gains the `charts/heatmap/` (weekly attendance plus a varied multi-class intensity-scale section) and `charts/radar/` (class competency profile plus a ten-axis six-class section) pages wired into the charts nav between pie and code-block, with light/dark/high-contrast stories. Follow-ups: H6 showcase + release.
