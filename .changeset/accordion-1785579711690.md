feature(interaction): add accordion component

Adds the Accordion disclosure container built on the Collapsible primitive.
Supports single/multiple expand modes, boxed and lined appearances,
APG-compliant heading/button semantics, and keyboard support.

Also extends Collapsible with `appearance`, `value`, and `icon` inputs and
controlled-mode behavior when projected inside an Accordion.
