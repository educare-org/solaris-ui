V2-E (chart fullscreen + combos + cards): `SolarisChartView` now accepts opt-in N1 `heatmap`/`radar` values (`isChartView`, `SHELL_VIEW_LABEL_KEYS`, `solaris.chart.viewHeatmap/viewRadar` in en-US/pt-BR/es-419 with fallbacks; defaults stay `line|bar|pie`) and the shell showcase documents them in a new combos section projecting `solaris-heatmap`/`solaris-radar` through the shell `<ng-content />` slot from the same series. Fullscreen leaves the artisanal showcase overlay: `actionRequest === 'fullscreen'` now opens `SolarisDialogService.openComponent()` with an `xl` panel (`min(1120px, 100%)`), `~60vh` canvas via the new `--solaris-chart-shell-fullscreen-height` token, extended descriptions and a visible totals table; focus trap, `aria-modal`, Esc/backdrop close and restore-focus come from the dialog, with ink-safe print rules. New `charts/cards` showcase page documents the free chart-card pattern (plain `solaris-card` slots, no new wrapper) with three Sales Report recipes (Revenue KPI + sparkline, Sales by channel + bar + table, Monthly target + line + reference line), wired into routes, sidebar, translations and the sparkline/code-block prev/next chain.
