Add the Avatar family (`solaris-avatar`, `solaris-avatar-group`, `solaris-avatar-profile`) with `SolarisAvatarPresenceService`. The base renders a photo with `onerror` fallback, deterministic mesh-gradient art (FNV-1a seed over `userId`/`name` driving blurred color spots across ten `--solaris-*` token trios and three seed-picked shape layouts — orbit, ribbon, cluster — with two-letter initials and a surface halo), `initials`/`icon`/`empty` fallbacks, fixed `xs–xl` sizes in px, `full|md|sm|square` radii via `data-*`, presence dots (`online|away|busy|`dnd` → solid danger with a white bar|`offline`, opt-in via `showStatus`) with a timezone-aware off-hours italic `z`glyph in the status color whose surface-base stroke cuts it out of the status dot (default`09:00–18:00`local, overridable via`workingHours`/`timezone`/`offHoursOverride`), badge-semantics counters (`count`/`max`/`showZero`, `99+`capping with full-value`title`, circular single-digit rendering that follows the avatar radius, `danger-on-solid`digit), a scalloped info rosette verified seal with a`text-default` check pushed outward from the edge, an organization slot, an action overlay button (`actionClick`), and thicker border states (widened primary→info gradient `active`ring, gray`loading` track with a traveling primary bar, both floating with a gap and reduced-motion safe). Every marker cuts a knockout separator into the avatar edge. The group is data-driven (`users`, `max`, `overlap`, focusable `+N`overflow with`overflowClick`); the profile pairs identity with title/subtitle/badge via `solaris-badge`, measured ellipsis tooltips, a wider identity gap, a compact avatar-only mode, and a clickable identity that renders a link when `href`is set or a button when`actionAriaLabel`is set (actions slot always stays outside,`profileClick`emitted, both modes supported). Accessibility ships composite translated`aria-label`s (`solaris.avatar.\*`keys in`en-US`/`pt-BR`), real buttons for every interactive marker, and `:focus-visible`rings; the showcase adds the`data-display/avatar`route (examples, status, count, overlays, group, profile, states, size-radius, installation, props) with mirrored translations. Follow-ups: photo editing/upload/crop, generative-art persistence, lazy-load, custom overlays,`table`/`calendar`/`file-upload`.
