Add the Avatar family (`solaris-avatar`, `solaris-avatar-group`, `solaris-avatar-profile`) with `SolarisAvatarPresenceService`. The base renders a photo with `onerror` fallback, deterministic mesh-gradient art (FNV-1a seed over `userId`/`name` driving three blurred color spots across five `--solaris-*` token trios, with two-letter initials and a surface halo), `initials`/`icon`/`empty` fallbacks, fixed `xs–xl` sizes in px, `full|md|sm|square` radii via `data-*`, presence dots (`online|away|busy|dnd|offline`, opt-in via `showStatus`) with a timezone-aware off-hours `z` emblem that never changes the status color (default `09:00–18:00` local, overridable via `workingHours`/`timezone`/`offHoursOverride`), badge-semantics counters (`count`/`max`/`showZero`, `99+` capping with full-value `title`, circular single-digit rendering that follows the avatar radius, `danger-on-solid` digit), a scalloped amber rosette verified seal pushed outward from the edge, an organization slot, an action overlay button (`actionClick`), and thicker border states (gradient `active` ring, gray `loading` track with a traveling primary bar, both floating with a gap and reduced-motion safe). Every marker cuts a knockout separator into the avatar edge. The group is data-driven (`users`, `max`, `overlap`, focusable `+N` overflow with `overflowClick`); the profile pairs identity with title/subtitle/badge, measured ellipsis tooltips, and a compact avatar-only mode. Accessibility ships composite translated `aria-label`s (`solaris.avatar.*` keys in `en-US`/`pt-BR`), real buttons for every interactive marker, and `:focus-visible` rings; the showcase adds the `data-display/avatar` route (examples, status, count, overlays, group, profile, states, size-radius, installation, props) with mirrored translations. Follow-ups: photo editing/upload/crop, generative-art persistence, lazy-load, custom overlays, `table`/`calendar`/`file-upload`.
